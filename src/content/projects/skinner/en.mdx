## About the project

Skinner is a SaaS platform for managing the clinical operations of ABA clinics,
covering patients, professionals, therapy programs, sessions, tasks, scheduling
and progress tracking.

I worked on the development of the platform, mainly on the API and the business
rules, and also took part in building the web and mobile applications.

## Key technical challenges

- **Multi-tenant** architecture, with data isolated between organizations.
- Modelling complex business rules in the backend.
- Access control based on the organization and the user's role.
- A decision engine managing the lifecycle of tasks and programs.
- Idempotent operations and concurrency handling.
- Scheduling and recurrence system.
- History and traceability of the changes made on the platform.
- Authentication and authorization across the application's different clients.
- **Stripe** integration for payments and subscriptions.
- Web and mobile applications consuming the same API.
- Automated tests for business rules and critical flows.

## Architecture

The API was built as a **modular monolith**, organized by business domain to
keep coupling low and make the platform easier to evolve.

Critical rules are centralized in the backend to guarantee consistency across the
application's different clients.

The web frontend and the mobile app are structured independently, sharing the
contracts and services exposed by the API.

## Engineering

During development, I worked on problems involving:

- transactional consistency;
- concurrency and idempotency;
- multi-tenant isolation;
- relational modelling;
- authentication and authorization;
- integration with external services;
- unit and integration tests;
- incremental evolution of an application in production.

## Outcome

The project gave me hands-on experience building and evolving a **SaaS used in a
real environment**, covering API architecture, databases, business rules, web
and mobile applications, payments and cloud infrastructure.
